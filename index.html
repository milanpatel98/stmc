Welcome to STM

A community where everyone belongs

St. Thomas More is a Catholic parish in the Diocese of San Diego, serving Oceanside and the surrounding North County community. Our church is open every day from 7:00am to 7:00pm — a quiet, sacred space for prayer in the middle of a busy world.

From our vibrant Vietnamese community to Y Cubed — Youth, Young Adult & Young at Heart — there is a place for you here. Come deepen your faith, serve the Church, reach out to others, and join a group.

"Christ is risen! Because of that reality, our lives, our world, can never be the same."
